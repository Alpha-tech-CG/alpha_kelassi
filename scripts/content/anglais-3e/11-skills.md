In the BEPC exam, you must **read** short texts and **write** short productions (paragraphs, letters, emails). This lesson gives methods for both.

### I. Reading comprehension

When you read a short text or an authentic document (advertisement, letter, article):

1. Read the **title** and look at the document to guess the topic.
2. Read the whole text once for the **general idea**, without stopping at every word.
3. Read again slowly to find **precise information** (who? what? where? when? why?).
4. Guess unknown words from the **context**; don't translate word by word.
5. For **True / False** questions, always **justify** with a sentence from the text.

> **📘 Answering questions** — Answer in **complete sentences** and in **your own words** when possible. For "Find a word that means…", copy the exact word from the text.

### II. Writing paragraphs

A good paragraph has:
- a **topic sentence** (the main idea);
- a few **supporting sentences** (details, examples);
- correct **linking words**: *first, then, after that, finally, because, but, so*.

Check: capital letters, full stops, subject + verb in every sentence, and verb tenses.

### III. Writing a letter or an email

- **Informal letter / email** (to a friend): *Dear Peter, … Best wishes, / Yours, [name].*
- **Formal letter** (to an authority): *Dear Sir/Madam, … Yours faithfully, [name].*
- Structure: greeting → reason for writing → main message → closing → signature.
- **Example opening:** *Dear Ali, I am writing to tell you about my holidays.*

### IV. Dialogues and conversations

- Use **question forms** correctly: *What…? Where…? When…? Do you…? Can you…?*
- Use polite expressions: *Could you…?, Excuse me, Thank you, You're welcome.*
- Keep the exchange natural: one person asks, the other answers, then continues.

### V. Summarizing a text

- Keep only the **main ideas**; remove examples and repetitions.
- Respect the **order** and the **meaning** of the text.
- Use **your own words** and the number of words asked.

### À retenir
- **Reading:** get the general idea first, then look for precise information; justify True/False answers; guess words from context.
- **Writing:** a paragraph = topic sentence + details + linking words; check tenses and punctuation.
- **Letters/emails:** informal (*Dear + first name… Best wishes*) vs formal (*Dear Sir/Madam… Yours faithfully*).
- **Summary:** main ideas only, in your own words, same order.
