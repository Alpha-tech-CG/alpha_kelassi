Building vocabulary helps you understand and speak English about daily life. Learn the words below by theme (English word — French meaning), and use them in short sentences.

### 1. Family and school
- family (*la famille*), parents, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, cousin
- school (*l'école*), teacher, pupil/student, classroom, lesson, homework, exam
- **Example:** *My sister studies at the same school.*

### 2. Daily routine
- to wake up (*se réveiller*), to get up, to wash, to have breakfast
- to go to school, to study, to have lunch, to play, to do homework, to sleep
- **Example:** *I get up at six and have breakfast before school.*

### 3. City, transport and directions
- city, town, village, street, market, hospital, shop, station
- transport: bus, taxi, car, train, plane, bike, boat
- directions: turn left / right, go straight on, near, far, next to, between
- **Example:** *Go straight on, then turn left near the market.*

### 4. Travel and holidays
- to travel (*voyager*), journey, ticket, luggage, passport, hotel
- holidays (*les vacances*), beach, mountain, visit, tourist
- **Example:** *During the holidays we travelled to the coast.*

### 5. Health, body and illnesses
- body: head, arm, leg, hand, foot, eye, ear, heart, stomach
- health: to be ill/sick, pain, fever, cough, medicine, doctor, nurse, hospital
- **Example:** *She has a fever, she must see a doctor.*

### 6. Environment and technology
- environment: pollution, waste, to recycle, to pollute, forest, river, climate
- technology: computer, phone, internet, screen, to download, network
- **Example:** *We must recycle waste to protect the environment.*

> **📘 How to learn vocabulary** — Group words by theme, write a short sentence with each word, and revise them regularly. Keep a personal vocabulary notebook.

### À retenir
- Organise vocabulary by **themes**: family/school, daily routine, city/transport, travel, health, environment/technology.
- Always learn a word **in a sentence**, not alone.
- Revise little and often, and reuse new words when you speak or write.
